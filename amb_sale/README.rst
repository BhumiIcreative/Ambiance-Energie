Amb Sale
============

- This module improves Odoo Sales with advanced features for better sales management. It supports multiple order types (Standard, Granule, Service, Stove), subscription handling, and counter sales with automatic delivery validation.
- It simplifies payment processing with better tracking, multiple payment methods, and precise discount and price controls. Improved product management includes supplier grouping, default supplier assignment, and enhanced search for easier inventory handling.
- A user-friendly interface offers tools like client monitoring, advanced discounts, and custom views. It also includes custom reports like "Demande d'intervention" and updates to standard sales reports, making sales management more efficient.
